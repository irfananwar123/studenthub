Spaced Repetition Without Complicated Apps

Spaced repetition means reviewing material at increasing intervals. You do not need a complex app to use the idea effectively.

What to do

The basic schedule

Review new material after one day, then after three days, one week, two weeks and one month. Shorten an interval if recall fails.

How to decide when to shorten

If you needed more than a few seconds or got the answer wrong, move the card back to tomorrow. If recall was instant, lengthen the gap.

Keep the queue small

A short daily queue is easier to finish than a large weekly backlog. Ten to twenty cards is often enough.

Mix topics

Interleaving related topics helps you tell similar ideas apart, which is useful in subjects like biology or law.

Where StudentHub fits

The Flashcard Maker lets you mark cards known or difficult. Use those labels to decide which cards to review first.

These are general study suggestions, not official exam regulations. Follow your institution’s guidance for exams and assessments.
