A Calm Exam Preparation Checklist

The final stretch is not the time to learn everything from scratch. Use it to consolidate, check logistics and protect your sleep.

What to do

One week before

List exam topics, mark confident and weak areas, and complete at least one timed past paper under realistic conditions.

Three days before

Review your mistake log and the topics you avoided. Ask a teacher or classmate about anything still unclear.

The day before

Do a light review, pack your materials, check the route and start time, and stop studying early enough to sleep well.

The morning of the exam

Eat something you tolerate well, arrive early, and spend the first minutes reading every question before choosing where to start.

During the exam

Answer the questions you know first, keep an eye on time, and leave a few minutes to check names, numbers and unanswered parts.

These are general study suggestions, not official exam regulations. Follow your institution’s guidance for exams and assessments.
