About the project

About StudentHub

A free, independent study workspace built with plain web technologies and designed to work on ordinary phones and laptops.

Why it exists

Students often need a calculator, a planner and a place for revision notes, but end up juggling several sites that require accounts or subscriptions. StudentHub puts a focused set of tools in one place and keeps the data on the device.

How it is built

HTML5, modern CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Calculations happen locally. A service worker caches public files for offline use, and larger collections are stored in IndexedDB. There are no paid APIs and no external font service. The site's own code has no analytics; third-party ad scripts are disclosed in the privacy policy.

What it is not

StudentHub is not an official university system, an AI tutor, or a guarantee of academic results. It does not check the factual accuracy of your notes or citations.

Privacy first

There is no login and no server-side storage of your study material. Read the privacy policy for details.

Get involved

Bug reports and suggestions are welcome through the contact page.
