Frequently asked questions

Is StudentHub really free?

Yes. All tools are free to use, with no subscription and no paid API. The project is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. Your notes, cards and progress are stored on your own device.

Can I use StudentHub offline?

Yes. After a first visit, the app shell and tool pages are cached by the service worker so you can open them offline.

Where is my data stored?

Small preferences are kept in LocalStorage and larger collections in IndexedDB on your device. StudentHub itself does not upload them. Third-party ad scripts may collect standard browser information as described in the privacy policy.

Can I use these tools for school submissions?

You can use the results as a guide, but always follow your institution’s rules and verify official grades and references.
