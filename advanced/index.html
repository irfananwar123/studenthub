Advanced tool questions

Do advanced tools need an account?

No. Every dashboard runs in your browser, and any saved data stays in LocalStorage or IndexedDB on this device.

Will these tools work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the advanced hub and its tool pages are cached by the service worker for offline use.

Are the scores official predictions?

No. They are planning signals based on the information you enter or save locally. Always confirm official results with your institution.

Can I use all eight dashboards for free?

Yes. All advanced tools are free, with no subscription, paid API or hidden upgrade.
