Marks Calculator

Track marks across as many subjects as you need. The calculator totals your obtained and maximum marks, shows a percentage for each subject, and reports both overall percentage and the simple average of subject percentages.

How the calculation works

Overall percentage: Σ obtained ÷ Σ maximum × 100.

Subject percentage: obtained ÷ maximum × 100. The simple average adds each subject percentage and divides by the number of subjects; this can differ from the overall percentage when subjects have different maximum marks.

Worked example

Example: Math 85/100, Science 72/80. Totals = 157/180 = 87.22% overall. Subject percentages are 85% and 90%, so the simple average is 87.5%.

How to use this page

  1. Add each subject with marks obtained and maximum marks.
  2. Select Calculate marks.
  3. Review the table of subject percentages and the summary.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

Why do overall percentage and average differ?

Overall percentage weights subjects by their maximum marks. The average treats every subject equally.

Can I use decimals?

Yes, decimal marks such as 78.5 are supported.

Are my marks saved online?

No. The calculation stays on your device; nothing is uploaded.
