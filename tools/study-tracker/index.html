Study Planning

Daily Study Tracker

Log what you studied and for how long, then see your effort add up across the week. Sessions are stored locally, grouped by subject and shown in a simple bar chart so you can spot consistent habits.

How the calculation works

Daily total: Σ minutes for that date. Weekly total: Σ minutes for the last seven calendar days. The chart scales each bar relative to the busiest day in that window.

Worked example

Example: 45 minutes of Maths and 30 minutes of Physics on the same day give a daily total of 75 minutes. Over a week with 300 logged minutes, the average is about 43 minutes per day.

How to use this page

  1. Enter a subject, minutes and date.
  2. Select Log session.
  3. Review daily totals, the seven-day chart and the subject list.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I correct a mistaken entry?

Yes, each session has a Remove control. Log the corrected session again.

What is the CSV export for?

It creates a downloadable file you can open in a spreadsheet; the file never leaves your device.

Does the tracker measure time automatically?

No. You record completed sessions yourself, which keeps the tool private and simple.
