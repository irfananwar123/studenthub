Interleaving Schedule Builder

Blocked practice feels smooth; interleaving feels harder and remembered better. Enter your topics with a confidence level and this builder creates a day-by-day schedule that mixes them, gives weak topics more slots and avoids repeating the same topic in consecutive sessions.

Use the tool

Use Topic|low, Topic|medium or Topic|high. At least two topics are needed.

How the calculation works

Confidence is converted into weighting: low 3, medium 2 and high 1. Each day contains available minutes ÷ block length topic blocks, capped at four. For each block the builder picks the topic furthest below its confidence-based share that is different from the previous block, so weak areas genuinely receive more practice without consecutive repetition. The mix score is different adjacent topic pairs ÷ all adjacent pairs × 100.

Worked example

Example: Biology marked low, Chemistry medium and Physics high, with 60 minutes a day and 30-minute blocks, gives two blocks each day. Biology is selected more often than Physics, and a Biology block is never immediately followed by another Biology block.

How to use this page

  1. List at least two topics with a confidence level after a pipe, such as Biology|low.
  2. Set the number of days, daily minutes and length of each topic block.
  3. Choose a mix style and select Build schedule.
  4. Follow the daily blocks, then check the time-by-topic totals for balance.
  5. Save the topic list as a local draft if you want to rebuild the schedule later.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

Why does interleaving feel harder?

Switching topics forces your brain to retrieve each method again. That extra effort is why it often improves long-term recall even though practice feels less smooth.

Can I use more than three topics?

Yes. Add one topic per line. The builder keeps mixing them and prevents immediate repeats where possible.

What if my daily minutes are not a multiple of the block length?

The final block is shortened to use the remaining time, so no minute in your daily budget is wasted.

Is the schedule uploaded?

No. All dates and totals are calculated in your browser from the topics you type.
