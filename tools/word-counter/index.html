How the calculation works

Words are sequences of characters separated by whitespace. Characters without spaces remove every whitespace character. Sentence estimates count groups ending in ., ! or ?. Reading minutes: words ÷ 200.

Worked example

Example: the sentence “Study smarter. Achieve more.” contains 4 words, 30 characters including spaces and 2 sentences. Automated counts are estimates; publishers may count hyphenated words or numerals differently.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded?

No. All counting runs in your browser and the text is never sent to a server.

How are hyphenated words counted?

As one word, because they are not separated by whitespace.

Why is the reading time an estimate?

Reading speed varies with difficulty and purpose. Adjust the speed in the reading time calculator.
