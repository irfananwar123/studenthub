Unit Converter

A quick converter for everyday science and maths questions. Choose a category, enter a value and pick the units to convert between. All conversions use published standard factors and run locally.

How the calculation works

Length, mass and volume convert through a base unit: result = value × factor(from) ÷ factor(to). For example 1 inch = 0.0254 metres exactly.

Temperature uses offsets: °F = °C × 9/5 + 32 and K = °C + 273.15.

Worked example

Example: 10 kilometres = 10,000 metres = about 6.21 miles. 100 °C = 212 °F = 373.15 K. 2 US cups = about 473.18 millilitres.

How to use this page

  1. Choose a measurement category.
  2. Enter a value and select the source and target units.
  3. Select Convert and check the precision note.

Frequently asked questions

Which gallon and cup are used?

US customary units are used for volume. UK imperial measures differ.

How precise are the results?

Factors such as the inch and pound are exact; results are displayed to six significant digits at most.

Can I convert temperature differences?

No. This tool converts temperature readings, not differences between temperatures.
