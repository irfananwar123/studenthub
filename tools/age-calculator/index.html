Age Calculator

Find an exact age for forms, records or curiosity. Enter a date of birth and an “as of” date, and the calculator returns completed years, months and days plus the total number of days.

How the calculation works

Borrow days from the previous month when the day of the “as of” date is earlier than the birth day, then borrow 12 months when the month difference is negative. Total days are the difference between the two dates in whole days.

Worked example

Example: born 29 February 2000, as of 28 February 2025 → 24 years, 11 months, 30 days in a non-leap year; as of 29 February 2024 → 24 years, 0 months, 0 days. The calculator validates real calendar dates, so 30 February is rejected.

How to use this page

  1. Enter a valid date of birth.
  2. Choose the date at which age should be measured.
  3. Select Calculate age for the full breakdown.

Frequently asked questions

How are leap-day birthdays handled?

The calculation uses real calendar dates. A 29 February birth date is valid only in leap years; results borrow days from February in non-leap years.

Can I calculate age on a past date?

Yes, as long as the “as of” date is on or after the birth date.

Is the result used for legal purposes?

No. Legal age rules can differ; use the official source for any legal decision.
