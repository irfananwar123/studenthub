Quiz Maker

Turn your revision questions into a quiz with four answer options. Add as many questions as you like, take the quiz, then review both correct and incorrect answers with your own explanations.

How the calculation works

Score: correct answers ÷ total questions × 100. Each question must have four non-empty options and one marked correct answer before it can be added.

Worked example

Example: a five-question quiz with four correct answers scores 4 ÷ 5 × 100 = 80%. The review then lists the one question you answered incorrectly together with its explanation.

How to use this page

  1. Add questions with four options and mark the correct one.
  2. Answer every question in the Take the quiz section.
  3. Submit to see your score, then review incorrect answers.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I shuffle quiz questions?

Yes, use Shuffle questions before taking the quiz. Your answers stay matched to the right question.

What if I leave a question blank?

The result counts it as unanswered and includes it in the review.

Are quizzes saved?

Yes, in this browser’s local database. No account or network connection is needed.
