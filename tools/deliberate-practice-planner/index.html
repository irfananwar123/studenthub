Deliberate Practice Planner

Deliberate practice means working just beyond your current ability, getting quick feedback and repeating the weak part. This planner turns a skill level and a weekly routine into an honest estimate of the hours required — and tells you when your current schedule is not enough.

How the calculation works

Each level step needs 3 + current level × 1.5 hours before feedback weighting. A coach multiplies the step by 1.0, a peer by 1.1, a past paper by 1.15 and a self-quiz by 1.25, because practice without feedback takes longer to correct. Weekly hours are sessions × minutes ÷ 60, available hours are weekly hours × weeks, and coverage is available ÷ required × 100. The weakest subskill is placed first in the session list.

Worked example

Example: Moving from level 6 to level 7 with coach feedback needs 3 + 6 × 1.5 = 12 hours. Three 30-minute sessions provide 1.5 hours a week, so six weeks offer 9 hours — 75% of the plan. You need about 3 extra hours or one more week to close the gap.

How to use this page

  1. Name a skill and rate your current and target level from 1 to 10.
  2. Add the weakest subskill, your weekly sessions, session length and available weeks.
  3. Choose the feedback you can actually get, then select Build practice plan.
  4. Read the required hours, schedule coverage and each level step.
  5. Use the four-session loop to start, and save the draft if you want to return to it.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an exact prediction?

No. It is a planning model based on consistent practice and feedback. Real progress depends on the task, your focus and the quality of feedback.

Why does feedback change the hours?

Deliberate practice depends on knowing what was wrong. Without prompt feedback, mistakes can be repeated for longer before they are corrected.

Can I lower the level target?

Yes. Set the target to any level above your current one, or equal to it if your goal is maintenance.

Is my draft uploaded?

No. The last draft stays in LocalStorage on this device.
