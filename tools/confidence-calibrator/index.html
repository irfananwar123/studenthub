Confidence Calibrator

Good students are not always good judges of their own understanding. Enter a prediction and a real score for each quiz, and this dashboard measures the gap, the direction of your bias and whether your estimates are getting better over time. It turns guesswork into a habit you can improve.

How the calculation works

For each quiz, error is predicted − actual; a positive error means overconfidence. Mean absolute error is the average of the absolute errors, and accuracy is 100 − mean absolute error. Bias is the average signed error: above +5 is overconfident, below −5 is underconfident, and anything between is well calibrated. The Brier-style score averages (error ÷ 100)², where lower is better. Trend compares the average absolute error of the first half with the second half.

Worked example

Example: Predictions of 85, 80 and 75 against actual scores of 70, 65 and 68 give errors of +15, +15 and +7. Bias is about +12.3, so this student is overconfident, while accuracy is about 87.7% and the trend is improving because the last gap is smaller.

How to use this page

  1. Before each quiz, write down the percentage you genuinely expect.
  2. After the result, add a line with the quiz name, prediction and actual score.
  3. Select Check calibration to see bias, accuracy and trend.
  4. Read the quiz-by-quiz list to find where your estimate drifted most.
  5. Follow the advice and save the draft to keep improving your prediction habit.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good accuracy?

Higher is better, but the direction matters more. A steady bias near zero means your confidence tracks your real performance, even if accuracy changes.

Why does the Brier score use squares?

Squaring penalises large misses more heavily, so one wildly wrong prediction cannot hide behind several close ones.

Do I need many quizzes?

Three or more give a useful first read. More results make the trend more reliable.

Are my scores stored anywhere?

Only in this page and, if you choose Save draft, in LocalStorage on this device.
