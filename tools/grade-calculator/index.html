Grade Calculator

Enter the marks you obtained and the maximum available to get a percentage and the matching grade. The default boundaries can be replaced with the exact cut-offs used by your school or course.

How the calculation works

percentage = marks obtained ÷ total marks × 100. Your grade is the highest boundary that your percentage meets or exceeds.

Worked example

Example: 78 out of 100 = 78%. With the default boundaries 78% falls between 70 and 80, so the grade is B+.

How to use this page

  1. Check that the grade boundaries match your course handbook.
  2. Enter marks obtained and total marks.
  3. Select Calculate grade to see the percentage, grade and distance to the next boundary.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if marks exceed the total?

The tool accepts the calculation but warns you, because the percentage will be above 100%.

Are grade boundaries universal?

No. They vary by country, institution and course. Edit the boundary box to match yours.

Can I use non-numeric grades?

Yes. Any label such as Pass, Merit or Distinction can be entered after the minimum percentage.
