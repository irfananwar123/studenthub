Study Planning

Exam Countdown

Keep every exam date in one place and watch the time remaining. Exams are saved in your browser, and countdowns use the date and time you enter in your own local time zone.

Use the tool

Results

Your results appear hereFill in the fields and choose the calculate button.

How the calculation works

The countdown subtracts the current timestamp from the exam timestamp: remaining = exam time − now. Both values are converted to milliseconds since the Unix epoch, so daylight-saving changes in your local time zone are handled by the browser.

Worked example

Example: an exam at 09:00 tomorrow shown at 09:00 today has 1 day, 0 hours, 0 minutes remaining. Once the exam time passes, the card shows “Time reached” rather than a negative countdown.

How to use this page

  1. Type an exam name and pick its date and local time.
  2. Select Add exam; repeat for as many exams as you need.
  3. Countdowns update every second and remain saved on this device.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

How are time zones handled?

A datetime-local value is interpreted in your device’s current local time zone. If you travel, recheck the exam time.

Can I edit or remove an exam?

Yes. Each saved card has Edit and Remove controls.

Are exams shared with anyone?

No. They are stored only in this browser’s local database.
