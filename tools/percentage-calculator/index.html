Percentage Calculator

Use this calculator for the three percentage questions that come up most in school and university work: finding a share of a number, measuring a change, and working backwards to an original value. Every calculation runs locally in your browser.

Use the tool

Enter +20 for a 20% increase or −20 for a 20% decrease.

Results

Your results appear hereFill in the fields and choose the calculate button.

How the calculation works

Percentage of a number: result = number × percentage ÷ 100.

Percentage change: change = (new − original) ÷ original × 100.

Finding an original value after a percentage change: original = final ÷ (1 + change ÷ 100). A negative change uses the same formula, for example 1 − 0.20 = 0.80.

Worked example

Example 1: 15% of 200 = 200 × 0.15 = 30.

Example 2: a price rises from 50 to 60. Change = (60 − 50) ÷ 50 × 100 = 20% increase.

Example 3: an item costs 120 after a 20% increase. Original = 120 ÷ 1.2 = 100.

How to use this page

  1. Choose the tab for the question you are answering.
  2. Enter the numbers exactly as labelled and select Calculate.
  3. Read the result and the short explanation below it.

Frequently asked questions

Can the calculator divide by zero?

No. A percentage of a number requires a non-zero base number, and percentage change requires a non-zero original value. The tool shows a clear message instead of returning infinity.

How do I enter a decrease when finding the original value?

Type a minus sign before the percentage, for example −20. The tool divides by 0.80 to reverse a 20% decrease.

Does this work with decimals?

Yes. Enter any decimal value such as 12.5 or 0.075; results are rounded to two decimal places for display.
