Exam Score Predictor

Use this tool to work out the score required in your remaining assessments to reach a target. It performs arithmetic on the numbers you provide; it is not a guarantee of any result and cannot account for grading policies such as capped exams or dropped scores.

How the calculation works

Target marks: total marks × target ÷ 100.

Required marks: target marks − marks already obtained. Required percentage of the remaining work: required marks ÷ marks still available × 100.

Worked example

Example: total 200, obtained 90, remaining 100, target 80%. Target marks = 160; required = 70 marks from the remaining 100, so you need 70% of the remaining work. If required marks exceed the remaining marks, the target is not achievable from these numbers alone.

How to use this page

  1. Enter the total marks available in the course.
  2. Add marks obtained so far and marks still up for grabs.
  3. Set a target percentage and check the required score and feasibility note.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a prediction of my final grade?

No. It only calculates a required score from the values you enter. Actual results depend on future performance and institutional rules.

What if I have already passed the target?

The tool says the target is already secured by the marks entered and shows how much buffer you have.

Can I include internal assessment marks?

Yes, include any marks that count toward the same total. Do not mix marks from differently weighted components.
