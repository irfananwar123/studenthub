Exam Readiness Score

One percentage rarely tells the whole story. This dashboard combines four signals you can control — marks, syllabus coverage, revision and consistency — into a single readiness score, then points to the weakest area so you know what to fix next.

How the calculation works

The score is a weighted average: marks 35%, syllabus coverage 25%, revision completed 25% and study consistency 15%. Each signal must be between 0 and 100. Bands are 80+ exam ready, 65–79.9 on track, 45–64.9 needs focus and below 45 high risk. The lowest signal decides the first suggested action.

Worked example

Example: Marks 90, coverage 60, revision 80 and consistency 50 give 90×0.35 + 60×0.25 + 80×0.25 + 50×0.15 = 74%. That is on track, but consistency is the weakest signal, so the first action is to log another focused study day.

How to use this page

  1. Enter your four percentages. Leave the exam name blank if you only want a general score.
  2. Select Calculate readiness to see the weighted score and band.
  3. Use Use my saved study data to fill coverage, revision and consistency from other StudentHub tools.
  4. Read the signal bars and next actions, then save the draft if you want to return to it later.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official prediction?

No. It is a planning signal based only on the numbers you enter or save locally. It does not guarantee a result.

Why are marks weighted most heavily?

Marks reflect assessed performance. The other signals show whether your current habits support improvement.

What does Use my saved study data fill in?

Coverage comes from the Syllabus Coverage Tracker, revision from the Spaced Revision Scheduler and consistency from logged habits and study sessions. Marks stay manual.

Is my draft uploaded?

No. The last draft is stored only in LocalStorage on this device.
