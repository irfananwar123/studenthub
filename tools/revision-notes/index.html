Revision Notes

A simple place for the notes you actually revise from. Organise by subject, search across titles and bodies, and export a backup whenever you want. Notes autosave locally while you type and can be restored after a reload.

How the calculation works

Autosave keeps the currently edited title, subject and body in LocalStorage as you type. Saved notes are written to IndexedDB. Search matches whole words case-insensitively across title, subject and body; nothing is indexed or sent to a search service.

Worked example

Example: create a note titled “Cell respiration” with subject “Biology”. Search for “respiration” or “biology” to find it later, even if the note is long.

How to use this page

  1. Write a title, subject and note body.
  2. Save when you reach a natural stopping point; autosave protects unsaved text too.
  3. Search or export your notes whenever you need them.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

Where do unsaved changes go?

They are kept in this browser’s LocalStorage and restored when you reopen the editor.

Can I export all notes at once?

Yes. Export as text creates a readable document; Export JSON creates a backup you can import from the workspace.

Can I use formatting?

This release stores plain text intentionally, so your notes stay portable and fast.
