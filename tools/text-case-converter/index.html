Text Case Converter

Change the case of a title, paragraph or list without retyping it. Choose uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case and copy the converted text.

Use the tool

Results

Your results appear hereFill in the fields and choose the calculate button.

How the calculation works

Uppercase and lowercase use the browser’s Unicode-aware transformations. Title case capitalises the first letter of each word; sentence case capitalises the first letter of the text and leaves proper nouns unchanged because the tool cannot recognise them reliably.

Worked example

Example: “study smarter every day” becomes “Study Smarter Every Day” in title case and “Study smarter every day” in sentence case.

How to use this page

  1. Paste text into the original text box.
  2. Choose a case conversion.
  3. Copy the result or swap it back for editing.

Frequently asked questions

Does title case follow a particular style guide?

No. It uses a simple word-by-word rule and will capitalise short words that some style guides leave lowercase.

Will proper nouns stay correct?

The tool cannot identify them. Check names and acronyms after converting.

Is my text saved?

No. Conversion is local and temporary.
