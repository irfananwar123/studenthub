CGPA Calculator

CGPA is usually a credit-weighted average of grade points. Add each subject with its credit hours and grade, then adjust the grading scale if your university uses different point values. Rules differ between institutions, so always confirm your own grading policy.

How the calculation works

CGPA = Σ(grade point × credits) ÷ Σ(credits). The sum is taken over every graded subject in the semesters you include.

If your institution calculates a cumulative average across semesters, add all subjects from those semesters as rows. If it averages semester GPAs instead, follow that published rule rather than this general formula.

Worked example

Example: Mathematics 4 credits × 3.7, Physics 3 × 3.0, English 2 × 4.0. Weighted points = 14.8 + 9 + 8 = 31.8; total credits = 9; CGPA = 31.8 ÷ 9 = 3.53.

How to use this page

  1. Select the grade-point scale used by your institution.
  2. Add one row per subject, with credit hours and the grade you received.
  3. Select Calculate CGPA to see the weighted result and each subject contribution.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the official CGPA from my university?

No. This is a general estimate based on the numbers and scale you enter. Only your institution can calculate an official result.

Can I use letter grades and numeric points?

Yes. The custom scale box accepts one GRADE,POINTS pair per line, so you can match most published grading tables.

What if I have pass/fail subjects?

Do not include them unless your institution assigns grade points to them; otherwise the weighted average would be misleading.
