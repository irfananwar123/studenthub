Flashcard Maker

Active recall is easier when your questions are in one place. Create cards, flip them to reveal answers, shuffle a practice round and mark the ones you want to revisit. Your decks are stored locally and can be backed up as a JSON file.

Use the tool

Your results appear hereFill in the fields and choose the calculate button.

How the calculation works

Shuffle uses a Fisher–Yates style random ordering so every card has an equal chance of appearing in each position. A card is marked “known” or “difficult” only when you choose that label; the tool does not guess your knowledge.

Worked example

Example: create a card with question “What is the capital of Japan?” and answer “Tokyo”. Flip it to test yourself, mark it difficult if you hesitated, then shuffle so it appears in a new position in the next round.

How to use this page

  1. Type a question, answer and deck name, then add the card.
  2. Flip the card and use Previous/Next to move through your deck.
  3. Mark difficult cards, shuffle, and export a backup before clearing your browser data.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

Do flashcards sync between devices?

No. Use Export backup and Import backup to move them manually.

Does the tool use spaced repetition scheduling?

Not in this version. It tracks known and difficult cards, but does not schedule future reviews.

Can I have more than one deck?

The deck name field groups cards; filtering by deck is available in the card list.
