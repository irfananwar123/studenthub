Syllabus Coverage Tracker

A syllabus is easier to manage when every topic has a clear status. Add topics once, mark them as not started, in progress or done, and this dashboard shows overall coverage, the weakest subjects and what deserves your next session.

How the calculation works

Coverage is done topics ÷ all topics × 100. Each subject gets its own coverage percentage using the same calculation. Confidence is weighted as low 0.4, medium 0.7 and high 1, then averaged across all topics. The focus list sorts subjects by lowest coverage first, with larger subjects ahead of smaller ones when coverage is equal.

Worked example

Example: Four Biology topics and three Chemistry topics, with three Biology and one Chemistry topic done, give 4 ÷ 7 = 57.1% overall coverage. Biology is 75% complete and Chemistry is 33.3%, so Chemistry appears first in Focus next.

How to use this page

  1. Enter a subject and one topic from your syllabus.
  2. Choose the current status and how confident you feel, then save the topic.
  3. Repeat for the rest of the syllabus. Saving the same subject and topic updates that row.
  4. Read the ring, subject bars and Focus next list to plan the next study session.
  5. Export the topic list as CSV or clear all tracked topics.
Everything on this page runs in your browser. Your entries are not uploaded or stored on a server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I update a topic?

Yes. Save the same subject and topic again with a new status or confidence and the existing row is updated.

Does confidence change coverage?

No. Coverage only counts done topics. Confidence is a separate quality signal shown beside the percentage.

Are these topics shared with the assignment planner?

No. Syllabus rows are typed separately, so your assignments stay in their own list.

Where are the topics stored?

In this browser’s IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded, and the export button creates a plain CSV on your device.
