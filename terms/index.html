Terms of Use

Short, plain-language terms for a free browser-based study tool.

Free to use

StudentHub is provided free of charge for personal study and educational use. There is no subscription, and no account is required. Advertising from third-party networks funds the site; see the privacy policy for the domains involved.

Results are guides, not official outcomes

Calculators, predictors and citation output are based only on what you enter. They are not official grades, predictions guaranteed to happen, or verified bibliographic records. Always confirm results with your school, university or style guide.

Your responsibilities

Enter accurate information, keep backups of important notes, and do not use the tools for unlawful purposes. You are responsible for decisions you make using the results.

Data and availability

Data is stored locally on your device. The live usage strip at the top is calculated from a small LocalStorage log of interaction times, tool names and page names on this device; it is never sent to a server and no global or cross-user counter exists. Browser storage can be cleared by you or by device maintenance, and the same clear-data control in My Workspace removes this usage log.

Third-party advertisements

Adverts are supplied by external networks and are not controlled by StudentHub. Clicking an advert takes you to a third-party site with its own terms and privacy practices. StudentHub is not responsible for the content of those sites or for advertiser claims.

Changes

Tools and these terms may change over time. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated terms.
