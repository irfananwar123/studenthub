Your data

Privacy Policy

StudentHub is designed so that your study data stays on your device. This page explains exactly what is stored and what is not.

What StudentHub does not collect

StudentHub has no account system, no analytics service of its own and no server that receives your tool inputs, notes, flashcards, schedules or study logs. We do not sell your personal information.

What is stored on your device

  • LocalStorage: your theme choice, favourite tools, recently used tools and recent searches.
  • IndexedDB: collections you create, such as notes, flashcards, quizzes, questions, vocabulary, timetables, tasks, sessions and exams.

This data remains in your browser until you clear it. You can remove it from the Workspace page.

Service worker caches

The service worker caches public app files such as HTML pages, CSS and JavaScript so the site works offline. It does not cache your private notes or other collection data into a shared cache.

Advertising and third parties

To keep StudentHub free, general pages load ad scripts from third-party networks: afders.org (pop-under), arwf.org (sponsored link) and bicea.org (native banner, social bar and display banner). These providers may set cookies or similar identifiers, record page views and measure ad impressions under their own privacy policies. A pop-under opens on every page view and a floating social bar may appear.

StudentHub also detects when those third-party scripts are blocked. In that case, or if a network returns no creative, it shows clearly labelled sponsored banners hosted on this site and a polite inline notice asking you to allow ads. These first-party banners are plain images linking to the sponsor; they do not set cookies, track you or receive your study data. Ads are not placed on the privacy policy, contact or workspace pages. You can still block the third-party domains with a browser extension; all tools will keep working. There is no paid ad-free tier.

Contact

Use the contact page to get in touch. The page does not transmit your message to StudentHub automatically.
